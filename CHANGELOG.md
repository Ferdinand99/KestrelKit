## Changelog

Releases and their notes are written by release-please from Conventional Commits.

KestrelKit speaks the app signals protocol v1 ([`docs/protocol.md`](docs/protocol.md)), which Keen
Kestrel implements from v0.2. Changes to what the protocol allows or what is filtered on the device
are listed in the release notes below.
